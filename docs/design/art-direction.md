# Art direction

Minimal, atmospheric voxel terrain with a restrained, slightly brutalist presentation. Mossland is olive, dunes ochre, highlands cool gray; foundation is darker neutral gray. Original flat colours, face lighting and fog provide legibility without Minecraft textures or UI.

The sky moves from pale gray-green at the horizon to muted blue-green overhead. Warm directional light and cool ambient sky balance material colours. Dense distant fog conceals streamed edges. Terrain silhouettes and colour shifts carry the composition; no decorative per-voxel objects are added.

The start menu uses a large system-serif title, quiet uppercase spacing, warm accent and dark transparent wash. Fonts are local/system and require no external requests. Gameplay UI is a 3px point, with no labels. Screenshots and measured inspection scenarios are stored under `docs/validation/`.
