# Interaction philosophy

The world responds through physical consequences. Clicking terrain removes one reachable voxel, reveals surfaces and changes collision. Clicking the foundation does nothing. No message explains its immunity, no progress bar labels hardness, and no text tells the player how to act.

The small centre dot is a neutral aim reference. It never changes into an interaction prompt or block identifier. There are no hands, tool animations, fake impact debris, inventory slots or interaction menus in this slice.

Do not add feedback solely to explain mechanics. Future audiovisual feedback may communicate consequences if specifically approved as a design change, while preserving the no-hints invariant.
